# local-transcription Specification

## Purpose

Offline, word-level transcription of a clip's audio into the Scribe-shaped JSON that the editing helpers read, on macOS and on Linux, with no API key and nothing leaving the machine.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Scribe-shaped output

The system SHALL write `<edit_dir>/transcripts/<stem>.json` (or `<stem>.track<N>.json` for audio track N > 0) containing `language_code`, `text`, `words` and `source`. Each word entry SHALL have `type` (`word` or `spacing`), `text`, `start`, `end` and `speaker_id` (`speaker_0`), with times in seconds and `start ≤ end`.

#### Scenario: One clip

- **WHEN** a 30 s clip with speech is transcribed with `--language en`
- **THEN** the JSON exists, has at least one `word` entry, every entry has `start ≤ end`, word times are non-decreasing, and the last `end` is ≤ the clip's duration from ffprobe

#### Scenario: Gaps become spacing

- **WHEN** two consecutive words are separated by a pause
- **THEN** a `spacing` entry spans exactly from the first word's `end` to the second word's `start`

### Requirement: Backend per platform

The system SHALL choose the transcription backend at runtime: the MLX backend on Apple Silicon macOS, a CPU backend everywhere else. A `--backend` option SHALL force `mlx` or `faster-whisper`; forcing a backend that isn't installed SHALL exit non-zero with the install command. The chosen engine and model SHALL be recorded in `source`.

#### Scenario: Linux default

- **WHEN** a clip is transcribed on Linux without `--backend`
- **THEN** `source.engine` is `faster-whisper` and the command exits 0

#### Scenario: Mac default unchanged

- **WHEN** a clip is transcribed on an Apple Silicon Mac without `--backend`
- **THEN** `source.engine` is `mlx-whisper`

#### Scenario: Forced backend missing

- **WHEN** `--backend mlx` is passed on Linux
- **THEN** the command exits non-zero and prints which backend is available instead, without a traceback

### Requirement: Spoken-language transcription

When `--language` is given, both backends SHALL transcribe in that language (never translate), using the prompt chosen for that backend and language (which may be none). When it is not given, the detected language SHALL be used the same way.

#### Scenario: Spanish audio

- **WHEN** a clip of a known Spanish script (e.g. generated by the local voice tool) is transcribed with `--language es` on either backend
- **THEN** `language_code` is `es` and at least 80% of the script's words appear in the transcript text

### Requirement: Cached transcripts

An existing transcript file SHALL be reused without re-running the model.

#### Scenario: Re-run

- **WHEN** the same clip is transcribed twice
- **THEN** the second run prints `cached:` and the JSON's modification time does not change

### Requirement: Language detected before prompting

When `--language` is not given, the system SHALL detect the spoken language from the first 30 s of speech before transcribing, then transcribe in that language with that language's prompt (or none). It SHALL NOT use one language's prompt on audio detected as another. The detected code SHALL be recorded as `language_detected`.

#### Scenario: Spanish audio without --language

- **WHEN** the synthetic Spanish test take is transcribed with no `--language`
- **THEN** `language_detected` is `es`, `language_code` is `es`, and the transcript's first word starts within 1.0 s of the first spoken sentence

#### Scenario: Explicit language

- **WHEN** the same take is transcribed with `--language es`
- **THEN** no detection step runs and `language_detected` is absent

### Requirement: Speech with no words is re-transcribed

After transcription, the system SHALL find speech from the audio's RMS envelope (the same speech/silence measure the short-form edge refinement uses). Each stretch of at least 1.5 s of speech that no word covers (a word covers at most its first 1.5 s) SHALL be transcribed again on its own (0.5 s padding, same language, no prompt) and its words merged in time order. Each patch SHALL be recorded in `source.patched` as `{"from", "to", "words"}`.

#### Scenario: Hole filled

- **WHEN** a transcription returns no words for 0–30 s of a take that has speech there (simulated in a test by removing those words from a backend result)
- **THEN** the written JSON has words in 0–30 s, word times are still non-decreasing, spacing entries are rebuilt, and `source.patched` has an entry covering that window with its word count

#### Scenario: Stray words don't hide a hole

- **WHEN** a backend result has three words in the first second and none until 32 s, while speech runs throughout
- **THEN** the stretch from about 1 s to 32 s is re-transcribed and recorded in `source.patched`

### Requirement: Untranscribed speech is reported

Speech runs that are still without words after the retry SHALL be listed in the JSON as `untranscribed: [[from, to], …]` (empty list when none), and printed as a warning naming each range.

#### Scenario: Unintelligible run

- **WHEN** a take contains 3 s of noise above the speech threshold that Whisper returns no words for, twice
- **THEN** `untranscribed` lists that range and the command prints a warning naming it, and still exits 0

### Requirement: Measured prompt choice

The prompt used per backend and language (verbatim prompt, plain prompt, or none) SHALL be the one that scored best on a recorded test set of at least 3 takes per language, one with fillers, measured on word recall, sentence-end punctuation, and seconds of speech with no words. The scores and the choice SHALL be recorded in the repository.

#### Scenario: Choice recorded

- **WHEN** a reader looks up the prompt for faster-whisper and Spanish
- **THEN** the code names the choice and points to the recorded table, whose rows give recall, punctuation count and missing seconds for each candidate on each take

### Requirement: Synthetic completeness test

The test suite SHALL include a Spanish and an English take generated with the local voice tool: 4–6 known sentences, one false start, and a 20 s stretch of speech after 5 s of silence. Transcribing each SHALL give at least 95% word recall, every sentence end punctuated or followed by a pause the captions break on, and an empty `untranscribed`.

#### Scenario: English synthetic take

- **WHEN** the English synthetic take is transcribed on the current platform's default backend
- **THEN** word recall against the script is ≥ 95%, `untranscribed` is empty, and each script sentence's last word ends with `.`, `?` or `!` or is followed by ≥ 0.25 s of spacing
